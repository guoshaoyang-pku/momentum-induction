From pixels to exact world predictions

A visual guide to the cellular automata experiments. The CA task observes eight frames of an 8 × 8 world and predicts the next eight frames. The separate billiards comparison uses a 16 × 16 world with the same frame counts. The figures below are rendered from the manuscript assets; the vector PDFs are available beside each preview.

Task and metricsThree propertiesModel designs
Reading the numbers. Sequence accuracy (SeqAcc) counts a rollout only when every predicted cell is correct: 512 cells for CA and 2,048 for billiards. Pixel accuracy counts individual cells. Unless a figure says otherwise, “unseen rules” refers to rules held out from training. These figures show selected reported comparisons; consult the paper and reproduction material for protocols, seeds, and model-specific exceptions.

The task and the metric

A cellular automaton has a fixed local rule. The model needs to find a matching observed situation and carry its outcome into the future. A strong pixel score can still hide many failed rollouts.

Information flow

What must be inferred?

For each queried cell, match its state and neighbour count to an observed transition, then reuse the observed outcome. L4 withholds a needed outcome and tests an additional inference.

Evaluation

Pixel accuracy can conceal failure

On held-out rules, the standard CNN scores 96.3% on teacher-forced pixels but completes 18.9% of full rollouts. The grey pixel bars use teacher forcing for most models and rollout predictions for the standard and causal-freezing diffusion rows; compare these bars only within the stated evaluation mode.

Three properties that make exact prediction work

The experiments isolate spatial locality, temporal locality, and temporal stability. They change where information sits or how predictions are sampled.

The three controlled comparisons

Spatial positions help a raster transformer find neighbours. Previous-frame neighbourhood features bring situations and outcomes together. Settled earlier frames help diffusion predict later frames.

Temporal stability

Keep earlier predictions settled

At eight denoiser calls per rollout and with the same weights within each seed, the frame-by-frame sampler scores 99.9% Game of Life SeqAcc versus 42.2% for joint sampling. Billiards changes much less under this sampler swap. The two samplers differ in ordering and noise updates as well as hard commitment. The figure's “Cannot skip rule steps” label describes this tested model, not a mathematical limit on CA simulation.
